# Workspace Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

A Workspace is the top-level collaboration boundary.

Everything belongs to exactly one workspace.

---

# Capabilities

Create

Rename

Delete

Archive

Invite Members

Manage Roles

Workspace Settings

Branding

Billing

Audit Logs

---

# Roles

Owner

Administrator

Editor

Commenter

Viewer

---

# Limits

Free

1 Workspace

Starter

5 Members

Professional

Unlimited

Enterprise

Custom

---

# Business Rules

Every Project belongs to one Workspace.

Users may belong to multiple Workspaces.

Workspace deletion requires confirmation.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

✓ Member management

✓ Permission isolation
