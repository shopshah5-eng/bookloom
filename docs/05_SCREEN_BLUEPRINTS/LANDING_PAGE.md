# BOOKLOOM LANDING PAGE

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Priority: CRITICAL

---

# PURPOSE

The landing page is BookLoom's public face.

Its purpose is not merely to explain features.

Its purpose is to make visitors believe that BookLoom is the most premium place to create, publish, and monetize books with AI.

The page should feel like entering a luxury publishing studio.

---

# PRIMARY GOALS

The landing page should convince visitors that BookLoom is

• Premium

• Trustworthy

• Beautiful

• AI-powered

• Built for serious creators

The visitor should know within ten seconds

"What BookLoom is."

"Who it is for."

"What they should do next."
