# Commercial Architecture Specifications

- Subscription & Tier Registry: Free, Starter, Professional, Business, Enterprise, and Custom Academic plan tiers with entitlement mapping.
- Billing & Financial Operations: Multi-currency invoice generation, payment gateways (Stripe/PayPal integration ready), usage metering, tax compliance, and automated receipts.
- Entitlement & Quota Management: Dynamic capability enforcement based on plan entitlements (AI credits, seats, storage, publishing exports, plugin integrations).
- Customer Success & Health Scoring: Automated onboarding flows, product adoption checklists, retention risk health scores, and lifecycle email triggers.
- CRM & Enterprise Account Sales: Sales pipeline tracking (Lead -> Proposal -> Closed Won), SLA contract management, and annual renewal forecasting.
